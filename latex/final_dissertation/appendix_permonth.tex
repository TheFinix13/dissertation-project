\chapter{Per-Month Test Results}
\label{app:permonth}

Wealth change $\Delta W$ in dollars for every held-out test month, at
seed 42, with $C_0 = \$10{,}000$ and a 5-basis-point fee. The
learned columns use the nine-feature state and the wealth-change reward
except the final one, which uses the risk-aware reward at
$\lambda = 0.10$ (Section~\ref{sec:res_risk}). B0 do-nothing is
identically zero and is omitted.

Two patterns are visible by inspection and are discussed in
Section~\ref{sec:res_final}. Months where REINFORCE equals the slice
schedule to the cent are months in which it executed an identical
action sequence, which is what a mask-determined policy does. And every
column beats buy-and-hold in the same months, namely the months in which
the market fell, because holding less is what they all have in common.

This table is generated from the committed results files by
\texttt{make\_tables.py} rather than transcribed.
