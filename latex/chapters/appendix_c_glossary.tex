Confidence signal ($u_t$) & A number from 0 (forecaster very confident) to 1 (very unsure), derived from how wide the forecaster's prediction interval is that day; also called the uncertainty score in the equations. \\
\addlinespace
Drawdown & How far the portfolio has fallen from its recent peak, expressed as a percentage; the risk measure pension mandates usually care about. \\
\addlinespace
Reinforcement learning & Machine learning where an agent learns by trying actions in a simulation and adjusting when outcomes are good or bad. \\
\addlinespace
PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization) & A standard reinforcement-learning algorithm that updates the agent in small safe steps~\parencite{schulman2017ppo}; used here unchanged. \\
\addlinespace
Forecaster & The neural network that reads recent price history and predicts tomorrow's return plus how uncertain that prediction is. \\
\addlinespace
LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory) & A neural-network building block for sequences such as daily returns~\parencite{hochreiter1997lstm}. \\
\addlinespace
Buy-and-hold & Passive strategy: buy at the start of the test window and hold; the fair return benchmark. \\
\addlinespace
Trailing stop-loss & Rule that sells when price falls more than $k$\,\% below the recent peak; common discretionary risk tool. \\
\addlinespace
MDP (Markov Decision Process) & The standard maths frame for ``observe state, take action, get reward, move to next state'' problems. \\
\addlinespace
DeepAR & Probabilistic forecasting method that outputs mean and spread, not a single point~\parencite{salinas2020deepar}; blueprint for the forecaster here. \\
\addlinespace
Sharpe ratio & Return earned per unit of volatility; higher means more return for the risk taken. \\
\addlinespace
Maximum drawdown (MDD) & Worst peak-to-trough percentage loss over the period; lower is safer. \\
\addlinespace
Value-at-Risk (VaR) & Loss level exceeded only on the worst 5\,\% of days (at 95\,\% VaR). \\
\addlinespace
Capital-preservation ratio & Current portfolio value divided by the highest value seen so far; target above 95\,\% here. \\
\addlinespace
Aleatoric uncertainty & Uncertainty about the \emph{market data} --- how noisy prices are today. \\
\addlinespace
Epistemic uncertainty & Uncertainty about the \emph{model} --- how unfamiliar today's data look versus training years. \\
\addlinespace
MC dropout (Monte-Carlo dropout) & Run the network several times with randomness switched on; spread of answers estimates epistemic uncertainty~\parencite{gal2016dropout}. \\
\addlinespace
Seed & Fixed random starting point for training; many seeds show a result is not luck. \\
\addlinespace
Walk-forward validation & Train on past years, test on later years, slide the window forward --- mimics real deployment order. \\
\addlinespace
Block bootstrap & Resample contiguous chunks of history to create synthetic training paths~\parencite{politis1994stationary}. \\
\addlinespace
Wilcoxon signed-rank test & Non-parametric test asking whether two strategies differ on matched pairs (here, matched stocks). \\
\addlinespace
ETF (exchange-traded fund) & A traded fund that tracks an index or sector; SPY tracks the US large-cap index. \\
\end{longtable}
