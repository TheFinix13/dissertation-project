\chapter{Glossary}

PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization) & A reinforcement-learning algorithm for agents that take a sequence of actions; it updates the agent's policy in small, clipped steps so that no single update overshoots~\parencite{schulman2017ppo}. \\
\addlinespace
LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory) & A type of neural network designed to learn patterns in sequences, such as a series of daily stock returns~\parencite{hochreiter1997lstm}. \\
\addlinespace
MDP (Markov Decision Process) & The standard mathematical frame for sequential decision problems under uncertainty, in which today's action influences tomorrow's state. \\
\addlinespace
DeepAR & A probabilistic forecasting architecture that outputs both a predicted mean and a predicted spread rather than a single point estimate~\parencite{salinas2020deepar}. \\
\addlinespace
Sharpe ratio & A measure of risk-adjusted return: average return divided by the standard deviation of returns, where higher is better. \\
\addlinespace
Maximum drawdown (MDD) & The worst peak-to-trough percentage drop in portfolio value over a period; lower is better. \\
\addlinespace
Value-at-Risk (VaR) & The loss threshold that is exceeded only with a small stated probability (here 5\,\%) over a single period. \\
\addlinespace
Capital-preservation ratio & The ratio of current portfolio value to its running high-watermark; the project targets a value above 95\,\%. \\
\addlinespace
Aleatoric uncertainty & Uncertainty about \emph{the data} --- how noisy or chaotic the market is on a given day. \\
\addlinespace
Epistemic uncertainty & Uncertainty about \emph{the model itself} --- how unfamiliar today's data looks compared with what the forecaster was trained on. \\
\addlinespace
MC dropout (Monte-Carlo dropout) & A technique that keeps dropout active at inference time and runs several forward passes, using the spread of their outputs as an estimate of epistemic uncertainty~\parencite{gal2016dropout}. \\
\addlinespace
Seed & A fixed random starting point for training; running multiple seeds shows a result is not down to luck. \\
\addlinespace
Walk-forward validation & An evaluation method in which the training and test windows slide forward through time, so the agent is always tested on data strictly later than its training data. \\
\addlinespace
Block bootstrap & A resampling method that draws contiguous blocks of a historical return series to generate synthetic but realistic training paths~\parencite{politis1994stationary}. \\
\end{longtable}
