\section{Full per-ticker Phase-2 results}
\label{app:full_per_ticker_phase2}

Table~\ref{tab:per_ticker_phase2} reports the complete per-ticker Phase-2 results across the full 70-stock universe. Each entry is the median across ten random seeds at 50,000 PPO time-steps per cell, for all three arms: Arm~A (baseline PPO, no uncertainty signal), Arm~B (aleatoric uncertainty) and Arm~C (epistemic uncertainty). The final value (FV) is the median terminal portfolio value from a \$1,000,000 starting capital; the Sharpe ratio and maximum drawdown (MDD) are likewise medians across seeds. The aggregate of this table is the headline of Table~\ref{tab:phase2_headline}.

The two patterns visible in the aggregate are visible ticker by ticker here. First, the baseline PPO (Arm~A) finishes within a percent or two of its \$1\,M starting capital on almost every ticker --- it barely trades. Second, both probabilistic arms grow the portfolio substantially on the large majority of tickers, with the largest gains on high-momentum names (NVDA, PLTR, AVGO) and the few near-flat or negative outcomes concentrated on low-signal or sharply mean-reverting names (CRM, TAN, BA, TSLA). The aleatoric and epistemic columns track each other closely, which is the per-ticker view of the ``source of uncertainty matters less than its presence'' finding.
