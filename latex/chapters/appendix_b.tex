\section{Market-sample composition}
\label{app:market_sample_composition}

The 70-stock market-sample test universe is pinned in \texttt{experiments/configs/dissertation\_protocol.json} under the named group \texttt{market\_sample}. It consists of 41 single-name US large-cap equities and 29 ETFs:

\paragraph{Single-name US equities (41).} AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, META, NVDA, TSLA, AVGO, ORCL, ADBE, CRM, NFLX, PEP, KO, MCD, JNJ, PFE, MRK, LLY, ABBV, JPM, BAC, WFC, GS, MS, V, MA, PYPL, XOM, CVX, COP, UNH, HD, LOW, COST, WMT, BA, HON, GE, CAT, PLTR.

\paragraph{Exchange-traded funds (29).} SPY, QQQ, IWM, DIA, VTI, VOO, EFA, EEM, AGG, BND, TLT, IEF, GLD, SLV, USO, DBC, XLK, XLF, XLV, XLE, XLI, XLY, XLP, XLU, XLB, SCHD, VYM, DVY, TAN.

\paragraph{How to read the table.} The probabilistic agent reduces the maximum-drawdown number on \emph{every single} ticker listed (the Prob MDD column is monotonically below the B\&H MDD column). On terminal value the pattern is more nuanced: the agent wins on the broad-market and large-tech tickers where the 2022 drawdown was deep, and on the high-volatility thematic exposures (TAN, META, AMZN), and loses on the low-uncertainty defensives (SCHD, VYM, JNJ, GLD) and on the persistent-trend single names (NVDA, LLY).
