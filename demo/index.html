The transfer test — live

 

Agents were trained only on simulated monthly episodes (calibrated on 2018–2022), then run unchanged on the months shown. Lines are cumulative wealth change in dollars from $10,000 starting capital, 0.05% fee per trade, mean of six training seeds. Exploratory companion to the MSc dissertation of Fiyinfoluwa Akano (University of Surrey, 2026) — the SPY panel is the evaluated result; any other assets are unevaluated engineering demos.
